Open Salamander: Working with Windows and Panels

Maximizing and Restoring a Panel

A panel can be maximized (the other panel is minimized at the same time) to provide more space for viewing files and directories. This is especially useful when viewing images in thumbnails view mode. When a panel is maximized, you can easily restore it to its original size.

To maximize or restore a panel:

  • Menu: Left/Maximize/Restore for the left panel or Right/Maximize/Restore for the right panel.
  • Shortcut key: Ctrl+F11.
  • Use the Maximize/Restore Panel button in the Directory Line.
