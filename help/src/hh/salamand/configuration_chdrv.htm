Open Salamander: Dialog Boxes: Configuration

Change Drive Menu and Drive Bar

Customize the Change Drive Menu and the Drive Bar on this page.

Dialog Box Options

Select drives that should be visible
Selected drives are visible in the Change Drive Menu and the Drive Bar. Click a drive letter to change its selection. You can also use the arrow keys to move among the drive letters and the Space key to change the selection of the focused drive letter.
Select drives that should be followed by separator
Selected drives are followed by a separator in the Change Drive Menu and the Drive Bar. Click a drive letter to change its selection. You can also use the arrow keys to move among the drive letters and the Space key to change the selection of the focused drive letter.
Documents
When checked, the Documents item is visible in the Change Drive Menu and the Drive Bar.
Cloud Storage Services
When checked, items for installed cloud storage services are visible in the Change Drive Menu and the Drive Bar. Use these items to access the local folders of those services. Currently supported services are Google Drive, Dropbox, and Microsoft OneDrive.
Network
When checked, the Network item is visible in the Change Drive Menu and the Drive Bar.
Note: if this check box is cleared, the Network item is replaced by the Network plugin (to change the visibility of its FS item, go to Plugins Manager).
As Other Panel
When checked, the As Other Panel item is visible in the Change Drive Menu. Note: this item does not appear in the Drive Bar.
