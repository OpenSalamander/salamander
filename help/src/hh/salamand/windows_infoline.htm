Information Line

The Information Line is a special area at the bottom of the panel that displays information about the focused item or the selected items.

You can:
  • Customize the displayed information in Options/Configuration/Appearance/Content of Information Line.
  • Click displayed information to copy the text to the clipboard.
  • Drag displayed information to copy the text to the Command Line.
